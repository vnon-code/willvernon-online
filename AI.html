// CONCEPTUAL_WORKFLOWS_&_RESEARCH

Flux.1 Dev Outpainting & Dataset Pipelines

Deep workflow testing around Flux.1 Dev models. This includes building custom automated pipelines for LoRA dataset creation—structuring bounding box croppers and prompt tag consolidators (`CharFLUXDatasetCreator`). I have also engineered custom outpainting noise bypass configurations inside ComfyUI to bypass standard canvas frame edge seam bottlenecks.

Consistent Feature & Character LoRA Training

Research into preserving structural features across varying camera vectors. Implemented multi-pass conditioning with face/hand preservation rigs. This pipeline merges FaceDetailer upscalers with low-denoise IP-Adapter configurations, allowing a single trained target subject to be consistently placed in wildly varying architectural backgrounds.

Wan 2.2 Next-Gen Diffusion Video Inference

Experimental tracking of state-of-the-art open-source text-to-video / image-to-video architectures. Testing localized motion vectors, temporal stability scales, and optical flow weights inside Wan 2.2 workflows, optimizing latency and hardware-bound VRAM configurations.

Creative Stance & Ideation Acceleration

We view generative tools not as complete answers, but as a robust creative multiplier. By linking text prompts to physical and spatial variables, we can rapidly construct 3D environments, test architectural colorways, and build context and moods in hours that would otherwise require weeks of asset modeling.
