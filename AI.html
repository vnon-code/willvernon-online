// FOUNDATION_ETHOS

Ethos: Artificial Acceptance

AI should be treated as a collaborator to enhance and improve elements of the design process from concepting ideas to refining outcomes. AI should not be treated as a competitor within the design industry. There are clear pitfalls in the ethics and regulations behind generative models being trained on artwork which has not been consented to by human artists. I have been experimenting with use cases of AI in design projects since 2022. The rate at which AI technology and tools are developing is crazy. I have used many different applications of AI throughout these projects and experiments: everything from direct video and image generation, using ControlNet to create interesting outcomes, and AI-assisted web design. I am optimistic that the truly innovative AI applications will benefit the industry.
