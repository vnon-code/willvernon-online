# willvernon.online

Portfolio of Will Vernon: design, music and AI work, shown as an app-like experience with live sound.

## Language

**Gate**:
The first screen of every visit, where the visitor chooses to enter with or without sound.
_Avoid_: Splash, intro screen, preloader

**Landing**:
The top of the site's one page, behind the Gate: the dot background, the project strip and the sound HUD. Scrolling here steps through projects instead of moving the page.
_Avoid_: Home, homepage

**Stem**:
One separately controllable layer of a music track (e.g. drums, bass, vocals).
_Avoid_: Channel, track (a track is the whole song)

**Sound HUD**:
The floating panel along the bottom of the Landing holding the stem faders, the DREAM and volume faders, and the track card.
_Avoid_: Mixer, player, sound controls

**Section**:
One storytelling block below the Landing (e.g. About me, Music, AI, Contact), with its own address. Sections sit on a solid panel; the dot background carries on, scrolling, in its margins.
_Avoid_: Page, deep-dive page, chapter

**Learn More button**:
The button at the bottom centre of the Landing that takes the visitor down to the first Section. It belongs to the Landing and leaves with it.
_Avoid_: Index drawer, Explore button, menu

**Project Sheet**:
One project's full view, opened from the strip or from a Section, with its own address.
_Avoid_: Modal, case study page
