Sound & Geometry

In TouchDesigner and Blender, I leverage real-time spectral analysis to convert raw frequencies into structural geometry. High frequencies map to intricate displacements, while heavy sub-bass hits deform spatial coordinate fields.

The VJ Console above demonstrates this reciprocal correlation. Adjusting the dials changes the digital physics of the system, directly mimicking the synthesis parameters used in electronic music production (modulation frequency, decay, distortion, and grain).
