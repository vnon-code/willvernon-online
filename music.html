Alias: vnon

Under the alias vnon, I construct electronic music, focusing predominantly on the architectural complexities of Drum & Bass. Sound design serves as the central anchor for my work.

Producing isn't a secondary endeavor; it's the direct source of my visual syntax. Designing synthesized bass modulations, mapping transient shapes, and establishing rhythm loops directly impacts how I program responsive matrices and vector systems.

Sound & Geometry [ DESIGN_WORKFLOW ]

In TouchDesigner and Blender, I leverage real-time spectral analysis to convert raw frequencies into structural geometry. High frequencies map to intricate displacements, while heavy sub-bass hits deform spatial coordinate fields.

The Audio-Visualiser Console below demonstrates this reciprocal correlation. Adjusting the dials changes the digital physics of the system, directly mimicking the synthesis parameters used in electronic music production (modulation frequency, decay, distortion, and grain).
