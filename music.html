Music & Audio

Exploring the relationship between high-tempo sound structures and generative architecture. Synthesizing frequencies to influence physical geometry.

Alias: VNON

Under the alias VNON, I produce electronic music, focusing predominantly on the high-energy, technical structures of Drum & Bass.

Music production is not isolated from my design work; it is the catalyst for it. My understanding of frequency modulation, transient dynamics, and spectral mapping directly dictates how I program real-time responsive installations inside TouchDesigner and Blender.

Soundscapes form the atmospheric foundation for my visual projects, establishing a reciprocal cause-and-effect relationship between sight and sound.
