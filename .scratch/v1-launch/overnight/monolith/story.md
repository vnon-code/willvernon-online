## The project
- Not a uni brief. Personal AI experiment, made in one evening and night: 10 Sep 2025 (first stills about 15:16) to 11 Sep 2025 (last clips about 01:35, V1 render 01:39). Trailer recut Apr 2026.
- The idea (site copy): see how close AI could get to a convincing outdoor-gear campaign using only generated footage. A fictional brand, rugged boots, stone landscapes, slow-motion impact shots, gorpcore look. "The results genuinely looked like something from a real campaign shoot, which was the whole point."
- Card copy: "A mock outdoor apparel campaign built entirely through AI. Exploring gorpcore aesthetics and how convincing a brand film could look using only generated footage."
- Look: white-out sky, pale carved stone and concrete monoliths, one black technical figure (hood, backpack) with red mirrored sport glasses as the single colour. Files call the brand VARKON (the logo, an X mark, appears in the film's storm shot); the site calls the project Monolith Survival. Ask Will which name leads.

## Process beats (from the file timeline)
1. Brand and world stills in Midjourney (prompts name "hyper-realistic fashion photograph of a hiker", "powerful cinematic landscape shot", "A lone VARKON..."): the figure, then the stone landscape, from 15:16 on 10 Sep.
2. Nano Banana (Gemini image edit) passes, 8 files, to keep the figure and world consistent across shots: wall steps, gateway, figure on spires, figure at the pillars, cliff, storm, boot. (Inferred from file names plus visuals; not documented in a write-up.)
3. Character sheet: VARKON_1 to 8, eight portrait variations of the same hooded figure, one per angle (front, profile, three-quarter, wide in the monoliths).
4. Animation: about 25 five-second clips in Midjourney video ("the shoe stomps the ground", camera orbit, ultra slowmotion, "the camera remains perfectly still", gorpcore fashion shoot spins, close-up of a hiker walking). Best takes picked from 4 outputs each.
5. Edit in Adobe Premiere Pro (project MONOLITH SURVIVAL): V1 cut finished 11 Sep 01:39, 34 s outcome for the site, then a 9.9 s trailer in Apr 2026.
