## The idea
- Research: copy-led campaigns (Nike Just Do It, Apple Think Different, gaming slogans: Nintendo, PlayStation, Xbox "Power Your Dreams"). Xbox's own "This is an Xbox" campaign was device-led; Will wanted people and community, not hardware: "connection, inclusion, and belonging".
- Visual cue from current Xbox promo material: starfields and ethereal space backgrounds.
- Five slogans weighed: "Wherever You Play, We're There." (too reactive) / "Play is Power." (abstract) / "Play Your Way." (generic) / "Every Screen is an Xbox." (literal, no emotion) / "The World Plays Here." (chosen: global, community, Xbox as the hub, flexible).
- Concept: the Xbox logo as a sphere that wraps the Earth; bold type leads.

## Process beats
1. Concept frames: Xbox logo wrapping Earth, heavy green, space (p14).
2. Blender: space HDRI, Earth model inside a spherical Xbox logo; logo split into four meshes (rebuilt by duplicating the logo 4x and deleting three quarters of faces each), origins reset to the Earth's centre, rotated on the Y axis in orthographic view; globe and cloud layer rotate independently; green line emerges at the end (pp.15-16).
3. After Effects: roto failed on the curved wrap; re-exported from Blender with transparency; .mkv has no alpha so exported PNG sequence, rendered via Premiere (p17).
4. Polish: new background pushed green, camera re-aimed front-on, logo exported grey so colour-corrected back to white with masks, time-stretching, Xbox startup sound plus AI-generated voiceover of the slogan, type rising in sync (p18).
5. Final pass: brighter lights, official white logo bottom-left, topographic overlay tried then removed, green toned down to match brand, freeze frames to extend, subtle dust overlay (p19).
6. Standalone logo-wrap GIF loop from the animation (p20).
7. Mockup base: contour lines rebuilt from scratch (clouds, levels, posterize 10, find edges, Colour Dodge) (p21).
8. Mockups: subway billboards (green underline under "PLAYS" instead of green "PLAY"), bus shelters (rotated 90 degrees), YouTube ad (real screenshot, keyed out the video area after the premade mockup looked fake), Instagram phone (pp.24-37).

## Problems met
Single-mesh logo would not split or rotate (wrong origins); rotoscoping failed on the curve; transparency lost in .mkv; exported logo looked grey; green too bright vs brand; topographic background felt cluttered; premade YouTube mockup unconvincing.

## Outcome
10.4s animation (1080p, with sound): Earth, then the logo wraps it, "THE WORLD PLAYS HERE" rises in. Logo-wrap loop. Static core concept, 2 bus-stop shelters, 3 subway billboards, 4 laptop/YouTube, 2 phone/Instagram mockups. Tools: Blender, After Effects, Premiere Pro.
