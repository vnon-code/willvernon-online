## The project
- Date: made 11 Sep 2025 in one day (stills from about 15:10, clips 15:52 to 18:00, Premiere autosaves to 21:01); recut or re-exported for the site 23 May 2026 (file dates). Personal experiment, no brief, no client.
- The idea (Will, ai.json): "the same concept as Monolith Survival but applied to deep-sea fishing". A mock campaign for heavy-duty oceanic workwear (waterproof jackets, thick gloves, industrial gear) against dramatic underwater and open-sea backdrops, "a brand campaign for a world that doesn't exist yet". Question asked: could AI make something that felt like a real brand campaign?
- Process: Midjourney V6 for the stills (bioluminescent creatures, dark open water, a lone figure on a concrete slab in the sea); Nano Banana reference images to hold the figure and outfit consistent; Midjourney video for 5 s motion clips (orbits, slow pans, macro of rain on fabric, a white whale leaping, white dolphins); cut in Premiere Pro to 44.7 s. ai.json also names Luma Dream Machine and frame interpolation.
- Prompt shown on the old site: "ultra-realistic cinematic photography of a massive bioluminescent white whale leaping from the abyssal ocean, glowing internal light grid, deep volumetric light rays piercing underwater, heavy particles, slow-motion bubbles, high-detail water textures, octane render, Arri Alexa style --ar 2.35:1 --style raw --v 6.0". The 2.35:1 is the old prompt; the final files are 9:16 portrait.
- Look: black waxed shell kit with red accents (red gloves, zip pulls), white concrete slab and fog, dark swell, whale as the one white shape.
- Problems met: not recorded in any source (no notes found). Do not invent.
- Outcome: one 44.7 s portrait film; 9 s preview used as the strip teaser.
