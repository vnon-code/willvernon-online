## The idea
- Take an AI-generated concept and turn it into a believable 3D space in Blender: a hidden spice-smuggling outpost carved into a narrow desert canyon, Fremen ingenuity with off-world touches.
- Started as "how can AI be a creative collaborator in early concept development"; it became a journey of learning 3D from scratch with AI as support. Will: "less about the tool and more about learning the creative process itself."
- Three interests drove it: 3D design, AI, world building. Context: Andrei Kurylovich's Aurora Machina (AI image to 3D), Thomas Dubois's Chateau Noir.

## Process beats
1. Summer/essay research: AI in design, ethics, literature reviews; design of the essay (ControlNet title page). (pp.3-36, context only)
2. Concept generation, Stable Diffusion (via CivitAI): compared base models (SD 1.5, 2.1, SDXL) and six checkpoints (BoomerArt, DreamshaperXL, JuggernautXL, leosamsHelloworldXL, PsyFiXL, CyberrealisticXL); LoRAs; prompt engineering (pp.39-48). Finding: models gave similar results, so prompt wording matters most.
3. Re-watched the Dune films for the visual language; prompt ladders: "brutalist" to "sandworn brutalist monolithic mega-structure" (pp.49-56). "Monolithic" made results abstract.
4. First idea "Lore Keeper's Vault" (brutalist archive) then Smuggler's Outpost. Final prompt: "A hidden desert outpost carved directly into the rock face, Crates of contraband, Concealed tunnels, rust-covered landing pad sits at the edge of the canyon, half-buried, shifting dunes in background" (pp.57-60).
5. Shortlist of four, one selected as the base concept (pp.61, 64). Missing: landing pad, deeper dunes.
6. Blender from scratch: terrain via displacement and colour ramps (pp.66-73); cliffs by geometry modelling and sculpting, done twice to learn (74-84); measurements taken over the concept in Photoshop (79); building with arch, windows, roof (85-91); final assembly (92).
7. Atmosphere: HDRI hunt (93), procedural sandstorm from a YouTube tutorial (94-97), ornithopter model and a second building with crates (98-99), volumetric dust (100-101), depth of field to hide modelling flaws (102-103).
8. Outcome: viewport GIF (solid, material, wireframe, render), poster-style presentation, project page, four renders, 10s animation (render over 6 hours, noise-driven camera shake), sound (ornithopter start-up, Tibetan horns after Mark Mangini's Dune score interview) (pp.105-116).

## Problems met
- Object scaling, material stretching and UV problems, slow renders, scene organisation (crit slides).
- Inpainting to add a landing pad and dunes looked disconnected; dropped it for time (p.63).
- Arch modelling failed first try; inset plus bevel worked (p.85-86).
- Good desert HDRIs were hard to find (p.93).
- Ambitious plan with no clear roadmap; shifted focus from AI to 3D. Will says he would have moved to 3D earlier.

Tools per slides: Stable Diffusion, ChatGPT, Blender, After Effects, Premiere Pro, Photoshop. ChatGPT also used for Blender workflow help. Post-project: clearer interest in 3D design, film industry, dynamic projects.
