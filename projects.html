Handheld Stories

A reimagined interactive digital exhibition for the British Museum's Netsuke collection, transforming traditional static displays into a dynamic, 3D storytelling journey.

UI/UX Motion Design Typography
Final Outcome

01. Interactive 3D Models

The concept shifts the museum experience from passive observation to exploratory interaction. I leveraged high-resolution Sketchfab 3D models of the Edo-period Netsuke artifacts to construct the foundation of an immersive web interface prototype.

Handheld_Stories_Process_1

02. Typographic Hierarchy

A strict typographic hierarchy was established using 'Noto Sans' and 'Osake'. This was crucial to cleanly separate institutional, educational information from the more expressive, narrative branding of the exhibition.

Handheld_Stories_Process_2

03. UI Prototyping & Motion

The final demonstration was animated and composited entirely in After Effects. I incorporated carefully rotoscoped interactions and smooth ease-in-out scaling to simulate what a live, responsive 3D web exploration would feel like to the end user.

03 /

Remnants

Concepting a lost civilization through invented visual systems. Blending tactile glyph creation with AI-driven architectural generation to form an atmospheric, surreal narrative.

Generative AI Typography Motion Design
Remnants_Outcome_Splash
Final Outcome

01. Found Type & Glyph Creation

Initially tasked with inventing a written language, I derived structural lines from physical marks and wear found in the environment. Finding letters within irregular damage was oddly satisfying.

These markings formed the basis of a unique typographic glyph set, assigning phonetic values to create fictional scripts like "Haa'sk" and "Cra'sk".

Remnants_Process_1_Glyphs

02. ControlNet Architectural Generation

I utilised ControlNet in Stable Diffusion, using the 2D vector glyphs as hard structural constraints. The AI model interpreted these shapes to generate towering, monolithic stone ruins, effectively translating 2D typography directly into 3D architecture.

Remnants_Process_2_ControlNet

03. Post-Processing & Audio

The generated stills were brought into Premiere Pro and After Effects. I slowed the optical flow down to 60% of its original speed to create a brooding, immense atmosphere.

Finally, the visual pacing was synchronised to an ambient soundscape ('Ambi Drift' by Bliss Signal) to invoke a sense of mystery and a calming presence.

Remnants_Process_3_Post

Powersurge

A collaborative visual journey exploring the exponential growth of computing power, translating Moore's Law and historical FLOPS data into a dynamic, data-driven simulation.

TouchDesigner Data Visualisation
Powersurge_Outcome_Splash
Final Outcome

01. Sourcing Data & Moore's Law

Created in collaboration with Suyash Sunar. We wanted to create a "Data Portrait", observing how technology rapidly advances.

We sourced datasets from 'Our World in Data' tracking global supercomputer FLOPs over 30 years, reflecting the exponential growth predicted by Moore's Law.

Powersurge_Process_1_Data

02. Semeer Data Painting

This exponential curve was directly integrated into a TouchDesigner network. The mathematical data drove the parameters of a spherical remap and particle simulation.

We later converted the network into an interactive experimental tool, linking parameters to sliders so users could explore the data painting process in real-time.

Powersurge_Process_2_Nodes
